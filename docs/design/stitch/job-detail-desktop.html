About the Role

Nimbus Labs is on a transformative mission to construct lightning-fast, ultra-resilient financial technology and smart-logistics infrastructure powering millions of daily merchants across India and Southeast Asia.

As our Senior Flutter Developer, you will take ownership of our core mobile client applications, bridging sleek material & Cupertino UX metaphors with heavy-duty native platform channels. You will work side-by-side with principal systems architects, offline storage engineers, and cross-functional product squads to pioneer fluid 60-120fps interactions and robust transaction pipelines under unstable network environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • architecture
    Architect, build, and scale high-performance multi-platform Flutter apps with a strong focus on modular code reuse and testability.
  • sync_alt
    Integrate native platform channels (Kotlin / Swift) for low-latency biometric authentication, encrypted hardware key storage, and custom UPI payments flows.
  • palette
    Collaborate closely with UX designers to deliver fluid 60fps micro-interactions, responsive tactile layouts, and robust offline-first persistence engines.
  • groups
    Mentor junior engineers, participate in thorough peer code reviews, and champion clean architecture principles across Bloc and Riverpod state paradigms.

Requirements & Experience

check_circle

5+ years building production-grade mobile applications, with at least 3+ years dedicated Flutter & Dart development.

check_circle

Deep expertise in modern state management (Riverpod/Bloc), asynchronous streams, complex rendering pipelines, and automated end-to-end testing.

check_circle

Hands-on experience with native Android/iOS bridging, performance profiling (DevTools), and automated CI/CD pipelines (Fastlane, GitHub Actions).

Skills Required

Flutter Dart Riverpod RESTful APIs Clean Architecture CI/CD & Fastlane Native Channels (Kotlin/Swift) Firebase Unit & Widget Testing
